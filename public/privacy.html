Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Landing Page Builder is operated under the AuruNex name. This policy explains how information is handled when you use landingpagebuilder.aurunex.com. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to antcodeslabs@gmail.com.

Information you provide

When you create an account, we receive your email address and information needed to authenticate you. If you choose Google sign-in, Google may share your name, email address and profile picture with our authentication provider, Supabase. We do not request access to your Gmail messages or Google Drive files.

We store the profiles, links, folder and tab names, page settings, and pictures that you choose to add. You control what you put on a page. A published page can be opened by anyone with its address unless you enable that profile's PIN protection.

How we use information

We use account information to provide sign-in, save and display your pages, respond to support requests, and protect the service against misuse. A PIN is stored as a salted hash rather than as the digits you entered. Browser trust and temporary unlocks use security tokens and cookies so a protected page can recognise an authorised browser.

Information stored in your browser

Authentication and trusted-browser cookies support sign-in and PIN access. The Home page also keeps recent and most-visited links in that browser's local storage. This history counts only links opened from Landing Page Builder; it is not your general browser history and is not synchronised to other browsers.

Service providers and external content

Supabase provides authentication, database and file storage. Google provides Google sign-in, and website icons may be requested from Google's favicon service. Paddle processes Premium checkout and subscription management; we receive subscription identifiers, status, and billing dates needed to grant access, but do not store your card details. The browser setup page may load Google AdSense; where ads are served, Google may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies. Opening a link you saved takes you to that external website, which has its own privacy practices.

Sharing and retention

We use Supabase to store account and page information and make published content available to people who open your page. Other people can see the contents of a published, unprotected page if they have its address. A PIN-protected page requires its PIN or an authorised trusted browser. Account data is kept while the account is in use; you can remove links and profiles in the builder and contact us to request account-data deletion. Service-provider backups may retain copies for a limited period under their own retention processes.

Your choices

You can edit or remove your content, choose whether to publish a page, enable or remove a profile PIN, and remove trusted browsers. You can clear recent-link history from the Home page or clear this site's browser storage. To ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, email antcodeslabs@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above shows when it was last updated.
